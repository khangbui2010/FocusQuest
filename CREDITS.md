# Focus Quest Music Credits & Attribution

All background Lofi tracks in **Focus Quest** are streamed over HTTPS from the external online archive repository:

- **Archive Base URL:** https://khangbui2010.github.io/FocusQuest-LOFI-archive/tracks/
- **Dynamic Catalog Source:** https://khangbui2010.github.io/FocusQuest-LOFI-archive/tracks.json

No MP3 or binary audio files are bundled inside `public/` or `dist/`. The extension fetches metadata dynamically from `tracks.json` (cached locally with a 6-hour TTL) and streams audio via the Chrome MV3 Offscreen Document (`offscreen.html` + `offscreen.js`).
